# Logic review of the game, 26 Sep 2026 (integration head 01dfb5b)

Three adversarial reviewers read the code without playing it and kept only what reproduces as a
failing vitest on the real modules. The tests are on branch `claude/review-2026-09-26`:
`src/proto/game/review.loop.test.ts`, `src/proto/review.vehicles.test.ts`, `src/proto/review.flow.test.ts`.
Each owner cherry-picks its tests, makes them pass without weakening them, and deletes the rest of this
list as it goes. Ownership is in the last column; "play" is the play session, "vehicles" the fix session
started for this review, "realism" the onemap-4/realism session.

## The verdict

The spine of the loop is sound: with no service a town holds steady; a two-stop line earns fares, the
town turns growing within two game days and builds; withdrawing every line makes it stall then decline;
money is identical at every speed; nothing can be built without the money; loop lines, calling on the
left, one-way roads, dual carriageways, trains keeping apart, reversing and single-track sections all
behave; saves round-trip everything the player can build. What is wrong is a set of numbers and edge
cases hung off that spine, four of which break the loop or the money game as a player would meet them.

## Suspected, not reproduced (main.ts and the DOM can't be driven by vitest)

- **Locking the phone while a village is half built saves it as finished** (`main.ts:755` sets `live` at the start of `bringToLife`; the hide-save writes `liveIds`; on load nothing finishes it). Loses the player's world. Fix hint: set `live` only in `lived()`. Owner: play. High priority.
- A bus that finds no room on load is silently lost with the money gone (`lines.ts:107`, `traffic.ts:571`). Owner: vehicles.
- Track and stations can't be bulldozed at all (`main.ts:941`); a mis-drawn £3,200/m main line is permanent. Owner: play, after the above.
- A line pruned below two stops sells its buses with no refund (`lines.ts:75`). Covered by bug 2's refund.
- The economy's "No route from X to Y" line problem is never shown; such a line earns nothing while its buses are charged. Owner: play.
- The goal card and milestones aren't saved (`goalDone`, `firstLineAt`), so every load shows step 3 again for two days. Owner: play.
- Real-region saves are named and pictured "Region". Owner: play.
- A two-stop line with buses 1% full makes about 12× its running cost (loop.md says about 4× for a busy line); tuning, after bug 3. Owner: coordinator (economy).
- No anti-bunching: buses on a line bunch and stay bunched. Design gap for V1.5.
- The coarse economy grows every far place for ever regardless of service, and a place jumps on activation. Owner: realism.

## Checked and found sound

- No service: stable for six days, no builds, balance untouched. Two-stop line: earns, town growing on day 2, builds. Withdraw all: stalling then declining.
- Money identical to 1e-6 at 1×, 4× and 60-minute skips. Fares = carried × 30 × £2, running = buses × rate per game day. Selling every bus stops both. No NaN.
- The town panel's word matches its numbers day by day. Map labels and panel residents agree.
- `purse.can` everywhere; negative balance only through running costs, with an alert; no game-over by design.
- Loop lines call A,B,C,A,B,C; A-B-C-B-A lines call on the arrival side only at their own stops; left-hand convention consistent through stops, doors and one-way roads; dual-carriageway stops pair and buses reach the nearside lane before dwelling.
- dt: same calls at 0.1 s and 1/30 s steps over 10 minutes; trains the same over two game days.
- Trains on a double line never share a block, never pass a red, reverse at each end, open doors on the platform side. Single track, passing loops, circular rail lines and level crossings covered by existing tests.
- Selling a bus waits until it is clear of junctions and stops; no ghosts. Stop ids survive a split that doesn't run through them.
- Saves: query round trip (`size=50`), migrations, autosave vs hide-save ordering, the snapshot covers bridges and types, one-way, slips, junctions, interchanges, industries, stations, lines, trains, purse, clock and `world.live`; leaving for the menu saves twice, harmless.
- Deep links and the setup build the same plan; `?map=exe` rewrites and reopens; deleted maps (`town`, `sandbox`, `place`) are all handled by `GONE`.
- Coarse vs live economy don't double count; portals on 50 km send no trips to places that don't exist.
- Bounded state: events capped at 500, purse history at 60, travel-time cache by stop pair; tile eviction and `arrived` bookkeeping hold (the #58 fix holds on failed requests too).
